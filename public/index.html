Getting Married in Ohio Made Clear

Serving Dover, New Philadelphia, Historic Zoar Village, Sugarcreek, Bolivar, Uhrichsville, across Tuscarawas County, and neighboring tri-county communities (including Stark, Holmes, and Carroll).

1. Schedule with Tuscarawas County Probate Court

Tuscarawas County Probate Court (101 East High Ave, New Philadelphia, OH 44663; marriage licenses via (330) 365-3244) issues licenses by appointment—bring valid photo ID, birth certificate, and proof of Social Security number (plus certified divorce/dissolution decrees if previously married).

2. Valid for 60 Days Anywhere in Ohio

Once issued, your Ohio marriage license is valid for 60 days and may be solemnized anywhere within Ohio.

3. Sign on Site & Return Promptly

We complete the license signing cleanly after your ceremony and make sure it gets returned promptly for recording.

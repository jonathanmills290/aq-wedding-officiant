Ohio Marriage License & Officiant FAQ

Quick answers for couples getting married in Tuscarawas County, Ohio.

Are you legally registered to perform weddings in Ohio?

Yes. Amanda Qualls is an ordained minister registered and licensed with the Ohio Secretary of State to solemnize marriages anywhere in the State of Ohio.

Where do we get our marriage license for a Dover wedding?

If either partner is an Ohio resident, apply at the Probate Court in the county where you reside (such as the Tuscarawas County Probate Court in New Philadelphia). Once issued, an Ohio marriage license is valid anywhere in Ohio for 60 days.

Can we write our own vows or keep the ceremony non-religious?

Absolutely. Ceremonies can be secular, spiritual, traditional, or include unity rituals and personal vows—you approve the full script beforehand.
